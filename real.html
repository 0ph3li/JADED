@ISIT-REAL?

a hand with silver nails taps an old coin on an imperial jade bangle with a silver peacock

jade is easy to fake and hard to fake well. glass, resin, bleached stone dyed green: they all look the part in a market stall. but real jade rings when you tap it, stays cold when you hold it, and is heavier than it looks. a bit like the right person, honestly. turn the sound on. tap the bangle.
